## Where REMind does not compete

Streaming the Muse is better served elsewhere, and REMind takes frames from these rather than replacing them: [muse-lsl](https://github.com/alexandrebarachant/muse-lsl) (BSD-3, Athena with optics since PR #228 of 2026-06-30), [BrainFlow](https://github.com/brainflow-dev/brainflow) (MIT, Athena since 5.22.0 of 2026-05-10), [OpenMuse](https://github.com/DominiqueMakowski/OpenMuse) (MIT classifier, Python 3.12 or newer, last push 2026-01-28) and [amused-py](https://github.com/Amused-EEG/amused-py) (MIT, the upstream of the BLE layer here; Athena decoder rewritten 2026-03-16, last push 2026-04-23). Source: [`verify_muse_ecosystem.md`](research/2026-10-uniqueness/notes/verify_muse_ecosystem.md) rows 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6.

The vendor already detects REM well with a closed algorithm: on Muse-S against PSG, five-class κ 0.76 and REM κ 0.85 ([Lanthier et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12782022/)). Its Dream Recall mode is a REM-timed wake alarm inside Smart Wakeup, not a cue meant to leave the sleeper asleep ([`verify_muse_ecosystem.md`](research/2026-10-uniqueness/notes/verify_muse_ecosystem.md) item 7). REMind cannot inherit that κ; its own figure is a binary REM-vs-not κ against GSSC and is not comparable (see [`numbers.md`](numbers.md)).

## What REMind adds, and what it does not do

Of the repositories inspected, REMind is the only open engine on a consumer EEG headband that combines a stable REM gate with non-waking cues behind an arousal guard and a calibrated volume cap, a seeded cued/uncued split in which uncued items cannot be scheduled, a blind morning retest, a JSONL log of every gate and cue decision, and replay validators that run without hardware ([report, part 1](research/2026-10-uniqueness/report.md)). That is a claim about openness, protocol and auditability, not about detecting REM better.

It does not stream or plot the Muse live, run NREM closed-loop stimulation, compute fNIRS or HbO, detect eye signals such as LRLR, export EDF or BIDS, or offer a staging accuracy measured against PSG. No night with sleep-time audio has been run, so there is no result yet on whether its cues do anything.
